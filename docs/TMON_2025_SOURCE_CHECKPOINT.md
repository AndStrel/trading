# TMON 2025 minute-print coverage — stop before outcome testing

The [frozen same-day carry protocol](TMON_INTRADAY_CARRY_V1.md) was written before reading 2025 prices. Public MOEX ISS TMON/TQTF one-minute trade-candle responses were captured in ignored `.trading/tmon-2025-source/`; the ignored source manifest `.trading/tmon-2025-source.json` has reproducible SHA-256 `05cfe825a69277c84b1243fb1c8084840853a174bf39b8a4efdaeb6b195a1b7f`. Each raw response is hash-checked on read.

Only **timestamps and row counts**, not prices or trade outcomes, were inspected for this checkpoint. Among 261 ordinary weekdays, 253 have at least one print. None has all 421 one-minute candles. An entry print in the frozen 10:01–10:03 window exists on **184/253** active days; a 16:55–17:00 exit print exists on **189/253**. Both exist on only **141/253**. Thus the frozen rule would have **69 entry cancellations** and at least **43 entered days without an observed exit print**. This already violates its zero-unresolved-exit gate; proceeding to calculate net winners from the 141 observable round trips would select on future exit availability.

**Decision:** do not score v1 profitability from this trade-print archive and do not widen its windows after viewing coverage. Missing trade candles do not prove there were no standing bids/asks or that an order could not fill; they prove this source alone cannot validate same-day execution. A fresh TMON idea would need historical quote data or forward broker bid/ask paper observations, with a new protocol fixed before outcomes. No orders were placed.

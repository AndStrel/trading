# TMON@/SPBRU weekday intraday v1 — frozen discovery protocol

This is a distinct calendar comparison to the already rejected 11:00–17:00 weekend rule, not a retuning of that rule. The economic question is whether weekday money-market repricing yields a same-day change large enough to pay a one-kopeck adverse tick on each side. A one-day book and zero-brokerage preview do not establish executable historical returns.

Use the seven previously cached, SHA-verified T-Invest exchange-source hourly candle pages for TMON@/SPBRU, UID `498ec3ff-ef27-4729-9703-a5aac48d5789`, from 2025-03-01 to 2026-09-27. This source was used for weekend outcomes; weekday returns have not been computed for this protocol. The data are not an untouched independent holdout, so even a passing numerical result would only justify prospective quote collection, never live orders.

One unconditional attempt on every Monday–Friday date in that range. Buy at the **open trade print of the 11:00 MSK hourly candle** (08:00 UTC), sell at the **open trade print of the 17:00 MSK candle** (14:00 UTC), same date. No signal, stop, day-of-week exclusion, overnight holding, or parameter search. A missing, incomplete, or zero-volume entry candle cancels that day's attempt. If entry exists but exit does not, count an unresolved exit and fail the protocol; never score only completed trades as if this were harmless. First produce a timestamp/volume/completeness-only checkpoint without examining weekday prices. Do not suppress apparent exchange holidays after observing returns.

Gross uses the two print prices. Base adds one adverse 0.01 RUB tick to entry and subtracts one at exit; stress uses two ticks per side. Preliminary one-lot broker commission is set to zero, subject to account-specific recheck. Returns exclude tax, actual bid/ask, queue, market impact, and any unverified fee. An hourly open print is not a fill promise. Mark the lowest hourly low from entry through 16:59 as a descriptive adverse-path proxy, not an executable stop.

Publish all calendar attempts, 2025 and 2026 results separately, and combined gross/base/stress win count, mean, sum, worst, and base sum without the five best trades. Strictly positive net returns alone count as wins. Numerical discovery gate: at least 100 completed trades in each calendar block and 250 combined; zero unresolved exits; base win rate >=51% and mean >0 in **each** block; combined stress mean >0; combined base sum without five best >0. A failed gate rejects this exact rule. No weekday/hour/cost retuning on these same prices, and no transfer of MOEX results to SPBRU. Even passing all screens requires fresh prospective bid/ask and order-fill evidence before any trading decision; no order is authorized here.

# Frozen protocol: SBERP volume-confirmed weekly continuation v1

Historical research only; no real orders. Hypothesis: a positive completed SBERP week with unusually high exchange volume reflects information flow that continues over the next month. This is a new volume-based mechanism, not the previously rejected price-only pullback or relative-strength signal. Fix rules before calculating outcomes; do not tune them afterward.

Use MOEX ISS SBERP daily OHLCV. Discovery period: security-level 2007–2014 (2007 listing/warmup). Later diagnostic: TQBR 2015–2025, separately, without changing the rule. 2026 remains untouched. Exclude weekend sessions. Group completed sessions by Monday-to-Sunday calendar week. At the first observed weekday session of a new week, inspect the immediately preceding completed week and the 20 completed weeks before it. Enter if (1) the prior week's last close exceeded its first open, and (2) its total volume exceeded **1.5 times** the median total volume of those 20 earlier completed weeks. Exclude the signal week from the median. Require the two most recent known week-start gaps to be at most 14 calendar days; never use future availability to decide entry. No condition on the entry-week price or volume.

Buy at the entry week's first observed open; exit at the open 20 observed weekday sessions later, regardless of intervening calendar delays. At most five overlapping positions, no leverage, each sized at no more than one fifth of marked equity. Completed entries only count as trade wins; mark pending positions separately. Model 0.3% commission and 0.05% slippage **per side**; stress slippage 0.1% per side. No dividends, taxes, lot constraints or guaranteed opening fills are assumed. Audit marked active-week returns and compare against the existing SBERP always-long 20-session control.

The discovery period must have at least 100 completed trades in 50 entry weeks; at least 51% net-winning trades **and** 51% positive active marked portfolio weeks; positive mean net and stressed mean, profit factor above one, positive sum after removing the best five trade returns, and positive mean net in every traded year. Report absent years. If discovery fails, mark the hypothesis rejected without selecting a new volume multiplier or holding period; the later period may be reported as a diagnostic but cannot rehabilitate the failed discovery. If discovery passes, evaluate 2015–2025 unchanged and require the same win, profitability and annual gates before any execution audit. Neither period alone authorizes live trading.

# LQDT 2025: same-day Investor-fee ceiling

The [screen protocol](LQDT_INTRADAY_FEE_CEILING_PROTOCOL.md) fixed the 0.3%-per-side conditional Investor-tariff assumption and oracle low-to-high calculation before prices were read. MOEX ISS returned 294 distinct 2025 daily candles on TQTF: **253 weekdays** and 41 weekend dates excluded by protocol. Three requests contained 288 overlapping, byte-identical candle rows; the importer verified equality before deduplicating. No weekday candle was invalid. Raw public response archive: ignored `.trading/lqdt-2025-daily-source.json`, SHA-256 `59fcc8000d43a3318ce0644befa6dd52e60f30830a3437543fde7b170e75fe9b`. Machine report: ignored `.trading/lqdt-2025-fee-ceiling.json`, SHA-256 `d0920551eb83f65ed38c13accac332939d72970f0bc074f1934d3e5068a35ead`. Reproduce with `npm run build` and `node dist/research-lqdt-fee-ceiling.js`.

Even buying each day's exact low and selling its exact high **without regard to time order or actual bid/ask** clears the assumed broker fees on only **2/253 weekdays (0.79%)**. Those two dates, 2025-05-14 and 2025-09-04, have unusually large daily wick ranges; the screen did not discard them. This is an optimistic ceiling, not a tradable outcome. Under the assumed Investor tariff and at most one long trade per weekday, 2025 cannot supply 100 profitable same-day trades and an unconditional daily-long rule cannot reach 51% net wins. Selective strategies or a genuinely different fee schedule are not ruled out by this test.

Do not transfer this result to TMON, which may have a different broker fee. The exact current LQDT commission, instrument-specific promotions, spreads, taxes and whether either anomalous print was executable at useful size remain unverified. No order was placed. Sources: [T-Investments tariff](https://www.tbank.ru/invest/help/brokerage/account/get-bs/tariff/), [MOEX ISS](https://www.moex.com/a2920).

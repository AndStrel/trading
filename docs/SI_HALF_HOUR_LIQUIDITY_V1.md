# Si first-half-hour direction to same-day close — exploratory fixed rule

Research only; no orders. Motivation: a [published study of historical RUB/USD trading on MICEX](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.finmar.2016.09.002) reports first-half-hour to last-half-hour momentum associated with liquidity providers' overnight-risk aversion. This is a hypothesis transfer, **not** evidence that a 2005–2014 pattern persists in 2025 or survives current costs. The project's 2025 Si period has already been used for other experiments, so this is an exploratory screen, not independent validation.

Use the already archived and hash-checked MOEX ISS RFUD SiU5 / SiZ5 one-minute candles for ordinary weekdays 2025-07-01 to 2025-12-10. Keep the existing fixed contract switch: SiU5 through 2025-09-10, SiZ5 from 2025-09-11. Never stitch price levels. Require every minute 10:00–10:29 and exact 10:31 entry and 17:00 exit opens. At 10:30, compute `close(10:29)/open(10:00)-1`. If positive, buy at 10:31; if negative, short at 10:31; if zero, stay in cash. Exit at 17:00 same date. One decision and at most one position per day. A missing signal window is a data gap, a missing entry cancels, and a missing exit is unresolved; never silently drop these days.

Reuse the Investor-tariff futures cost model from [Si first-hour v1](SI_FIRST_HOUR_V1.md): 0.10% commission of notional on **each** side, plus 0.05% adverse slippage per side in base and 0.10% in stress. Calculate long and short fills multiplicatively, subtract both commissions, divide by entry notional. Gross is diagnostic only. Do not assume leverage return on margin deposit, live bid/ask, or guaranteed execution. No stop is defined, so a positive screen would still require separate risk-controlled replay.

Screen gate: at least 100 completed trades on 50 dates, no unresolved positions or unexplained data gaps, at least 51% strictly positive base and stress net trades, positive mean net in both cost cases and in each contract separately, positive result after removing the five best dates. Missing fee/spread/tax/fill evidence blocks live adoption. A passing 2025 exploratory screen would require unchanged independent forward validation.

# Frozen protocol: SBERP pullback within an uptrend v1

Local price-only research, never a live order rule. This deliberately tests a different causal combination: a short-term weekly decline may mean-revert when a longer trend remains positive. Previous SBERP reversal and trend tests informed the mechanism, so 2015–2025 is exploratory rather than independent evidence. Do not tune after observing the result.

Instrument: SBERP only, MOEX ISS TQBR daily candles 2015–2025, excluding 2026. On the first observed weekday session of each week, use only completed daily bars. Require: (1) the last close of the immediately preceding week is at most 98.5% of the last close of the week before it; (2) the preceding close is above the arithmetic mean of the last 200 completed daily closes, including that preceding close; (3) neither of the two known preceding week-start gaps is over 14 calendar days. Enter at the current first-weekday-session open. Exit at the open 20 observed weekday sessions later, without assuming future session availability. Five concurrent positions maximum; size each at no more than one fifth of marked equity. Do not place new entries when available cash cannot fund the allocation. Pending end-of-dataset positions are marked but not counted as completed wins.

Base costs are 0.3% commission and 0.05% slippage **per side**; stress slippage is 0.1% per side. No leverage. Compare with the already reported SBERP always-long 20-session control. Include portfolio marked weekly outcomes and 2016–2019, 2020–2022, 2023–2025 cohorts.

Minimum exploratory admission: 100 completed trades in at least 50 entry weeks; >=51% net-winning trades and >=51% positive active marked portfolio weeks; positive mean net return and stress mean, profit factor >1, positive sum of trade returns without the best five, positive mean net trade return in every traded year and each cohort, and no unobserved year. Any apparent pass still requires an untouched independent period, corporate-action/dividend and execution audits, and risk review before consideration of real trading.

# Frozen protocol: LQDT carry persistence v1

Research only, no order authorization. LQDT is a money-market ETF unit, not a guaranteed deposit. Hypothesis: when its last completed 20 eligible sessions appreciated more than the modeled round-trip dealing cost, another 20-session holding period may yield positive net returns more often than 51% of weekly entries. This tests rate/carry persistence, not stock-price reversal. The buy-and-hold benchmark matters: frequent rolling purchases are economically pointless if a continuous position is better at acceptable risk.

Source: verified MOEX ISS TQTF daily LQDT candles 2022-07-22 through 2025-12-30. TQTF begins only in July 2022; no 2020–2021 TQTF backfill is allowed. Exclude weekend sessions. Use 2022 only as lagged warmup. Freeze the rule before seeing outcomes: 2023–2024 is discovery and 2025 is a separate untouched **instrument-specific** validation period. Do not use 2026. Compute each period as a separate portfolio starting with ₽1m, with the preceding source history available only for lagged signals.

At the first observed weekday session of each new calendar week from the evaluation period, inspect only preceding completed sessions. Require at least 21 preceding eligible sessions and a prior close at most 7 calendar days old. Let `c1` be the last completed close and `c21` the close 20 eligible sessions before `c1`. Enter only when `c1/c21` exceeds the exact gross price ratio needed to break even after the fixed base dealing costs: `[(1+0.0005)(1+0.003)] / [(1-0.0005)(1-0.003)]`. This is approximately 0.7% appreciation, but code must use the formula, not a rounded threshold. Buy LQDT at the current opening price; exit at the open 20 observed weekday sessions later. Future session availability cannot suppress an entry. At most five overlapping positions, with each principal capped at one fifth of current marked equity and available cash. No leverage. Pending positions at the period end are marked but not counted as completed wins.

Base costs: 0.3% commission plus 0.05% slippage on **each** side; stress uses 0.1% slippage per side. These are conservative assumptions, not an account-specific confirmed broker tariff. Include active-week marked portfolio outcomes, maximum drawdown, positive and negative calendar-year results, comparison with same-weekly-entry always-long LQDT and a one-time buy-and-hold benchmark with just one entry and one exit cost. Mark out-of-window exits honestly; do not silently change a 20-session exit to fit the period boundary.

The full 2023–2025 combined sample must contain >=100 completed trades in >=50 entry weeks; discovery and validation each must show >=51% net-winning completed trades **and** >=51% positive active marked weeks, positive mean net and stress mean, profit factor >1, positive sum without the best five trades, positive modeled ending equity after costs, and no negative year. The 2025 validation must pass without changing the rule or cost model after observing 2023–2024. Even if those gates pass, the strategy is not recommended unless it beats the continuous buy-and-hold benchmark on a risk-adjusted and after-cost basis and passes tariff, spread, lot, tax, fund-risk and data-use-rights audits. No real orders.

# TMON last-hour same-day carry v2 — rejected

**Execution-grid correction (28 September 2026):** the provisional 0.000001-RUB tick below was too favorable relative to the current 0.01-RUB instrument tick and the cent-grid archive. The wider exit window removed **printed-exit gaps**: 184 completed dates in 2025 and 152 in 2026 through 25 September, with zero entered dates lacking a printed exit. It did **not** prove that the historical trade prices were executable at the required size.

- **2025:** 82/184 strictly positive base-net trades (**44.6%**); 78/184 stress-net (**42.4%**). Mean per trade: gross −0.0011%, base net **−0.0111%**, stress net **−0.0211%**. Base result after removing five best trades: −3.23 percentage points summed. January–June base mean was slightly positive (+0.0031%), but July–December was negative (−0.0204%). Inter-trade base equity drawdown proxy 3.09%; this excludes intraday mark-to-market risk.
- **2026 to 25 September:** 54/152 base-net winners (**35.5%**); 39/152 stress-net (**25.7%**). Mean per trade: gross +0.0029%, base net **−0.0071%**, stress net **−0.0171%**. Both calendar parts' base means are negative, and removing the five best trades leaves −1.37 percentage points summed. Inter-trade base equity drawdown proxy 1.28%, again not the intraday maximum.

The v2 rule fails the fixed ≥51% net-win and positive-mean screens **in both periods**, even under conditional zero broker commission. Its apparent first-half 2025 improvement does not justify selecting only that half after viewing results. A simple continuous-hold *price-print proxy* over each period rose about +18.8% in 2025 and +10.0% in the available 2026 interval, but it has different exposure and risk from repeated intraday positions; this is context, not a risk-adjusted performance comparison.

**Decision:** reject this specific unconditional same-day TMON carry rule. Do not narrow days or retune its exit window against these results. Actual broker fee, contemporaneous spread, market impact, historical tick/lot details, fills, taxes and real intraday drawdown remain unverified. Even favorable historical print results would not have authorized live trading; none were placed.

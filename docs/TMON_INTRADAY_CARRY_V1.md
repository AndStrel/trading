# TMON same-day money-market carry v1 — protocol frozen before outcomes

Research only; no orders. Hypothesis: a money-market fund's accrued daily income may appear in its same-session traded price often enough to exceed the cost of entering and exiting within the same day. This is a different economic mechanism from directional stock/futures momentum. The null is that spread/price noise and foregone overnight accrual dominate. Even a positive result may be economically inferior to continuous fund holding; report that benchmark separately before considering deployment.

Source: public MOEX ISS TMON/TQTF one-minute trade candles, ordinary weekdays of 2025, 10:00–17:00 MSK. Preserve raw responses and hashes. Weekend/special sessions are outside this rule. Do not silently drop inactive dates. No model fitting or parameter sweep.

For each active weekday, make exactly one unconditional long attempt: buy at the **open** of the first traded minute 10:01–10:03; if absent, cancel the day. Sell at the open of the first traded minute 16:55–17:00 of the **same date**; if absent, mark unresolved, never score a winner. No leverage, short, or planned overnight carry. No stop is inferred from sparse prints; audit worst-day losses and dates when exit is unavailable. The entry rule does not know any later price or future candle availability.

Current T-Invest published terms describe TMON purchases and sales through the broker without a transaction commission. Model zero broker fee **conditionally**, not as historical proof or an account-specific guarantee. Fund-level expenses are embedded in observed traded prices. Use the published TMON 0.000001 RUB price step provisionally, but verify against actual historical prices; round adverse buys up and sells down. Fixed adverse execution scenarios: base **0.005% per side**, stress **0.010% per side**, severe **0.030% per side**. These are unmeasured allowances, not observed bid/ask spreads; no real order can be considered before forward broker quotes and paper fills validate them.

Admission screen: at least 100 completed trades on 100 dates; zero unresolved exits; ≥51% strictly positive **net** returns; positive mean/aggregate base and stress net; positive base and stress means in both January–June and July–December; positive sum after removing five best completed trades; no economically unacceptable tail. Severe cost is reported as sensitivity and cannot be hidden. A passing 2025 screen only permits unchanged independent 2024 and 2026 historical checks, comparison with continuous holding, tariff and live spread verification, and an explicit tax/capital-use review. Historical prints alone never authorize live trading.

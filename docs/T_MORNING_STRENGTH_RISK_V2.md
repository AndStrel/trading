# TCSG/T morning strength with fixed stop/target v2 — locked before 2024 outcome

Research only; no orders. The 2025 v1 result showed a small conditional-Pro average that disappeared under stress and after removing five best trades, with a large adverse tail. The **new mechanism** is a fixed downside stop paired with a larger target, not a search over stop levels. This v2 was chosen after viewing 2025 v1, so it is sequential research. The untouched 2024 Russian-share TCSG→T archive is the first test; 2025 is no longer an independent holdout. No retuning on either year.

Source: 2024 MOEX ISS TQBR one-minute trade candles 10:00–17:00 MSK; TCSG from 2024-03-18 through 2024-11-27, T from 2024-11-28. Exclude no ordinary active date based on future outcome. Require exact 10:00 and 10:30 candles. If `close10:30 / close10:00 - 1 >= +0.002`, enter long at the open of the first traded minute 10:31–10:33; otherwise cash. Missing entry cancels the attempt. No short, leverage, or planned overnight carry.

From the entry minute onward, scan complete one-minute candles in order. Exit on first of: 0.80% adverse stop, 1.50% favorable target, or first open 16:55–17:00. If both levels are crossed in one minute, count stop first. A triggered stop fills no better than its level and at the minute open if the open has gapped below the stop; a target fills at its level, never a better price. Missing any minute before exit makes the attempt unresolved. A missing 16:55–17:00 print is unresolved. A candle high/low is **not** proof of actual stop/target execution; this is a conservative screening model, not an order simulator.

Two fixed cost scenarios, both with 0.05% adverse execution per side base and 0.10% per side stress: Investor adds 0.30% commission per side; conditional Pro adds zero brokerage commission within the published first-15-daily-eligible-trades benefit. Round buys up and sells down to observed 2024 price grids (0.10 RUB TCSG, 0.02 RUB T) before applying commission. These grids, historical real quote spreads, subscription costs, account eligibility, taxes and actual fills remain to be audited. Pro is paid unless already held; do not treat its benefit as unconditional.

2024 screening gate per scenario: zero cancelled/unresolved attempts; ≥51% base-net winners, positive base and stressed mean, positive aggregate without the best five trades, positive result in both March–June and July–December cohorts, no dependence on one date/tail event. If 2024 fails, reject without checking 2025 v2. If 2024 passes, run unchanged on 2025 and require a combined ≥100 trades across ≥50 dates with the same gates; 2025 is a contaminated diagnostic because its v1 result motivated v2. Any historical success would still need new 2026/forward bid/ask paper evidence, Pro subscription/tariff verification, corporate-action audit and explicit risk controls before a real order.

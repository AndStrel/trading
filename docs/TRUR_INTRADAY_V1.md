# TRUR same-day long v1 — rule frozen before 2025 price outcomes

Research only, no orders. Hypothesis: the diversified TRUR fund may have a small positive intraday drift that is not overwhelmed by trading friction when broker commission is zero for the first 15 filled orders per day. The published T-Investments rule is conditional on this daily shared allowance and can change; verify the actual account/order fee before any real trade. Fund expenses are already embedded in market prices. TMON and other fund outcomes do not validate TRUR.

Use public MOEX ISS TRUR/TQTF one-minute trade candles for every ordinary weekday in 2025, 10:00–17:00 MSK. Preserve raw pages with hashes and report all inactive and missing dates. Make one unconditional long attempt per active weekday: buy at the **open of the first traded minute 10:01–10:03**; if none, cancel before entering. Sell at the **open of the first traded minute 16:55–17:00 on the same date**; if none, mark an unresolved position, not a win. No leverage, shorting or planned overnight holding. Do not condition entry on later exit availability or tune windows after seeing outcomes.

Model zero broker commission **only conditionally**. Price-print fills are optimistic because they do not provide contemporaneous executable bid/ask or depth. Fixed adverse execution allowances: base 0.02% per side, stress 0.05% per side, severe 0.10% per side, applied multiplicatively to entry/exit opens. Report gross as a diagnostic. Do not assert historical tick rounding without verified historical price steps; the current TQBR step is not proof of the 2025 TQTF step. This lack of historical tick and quote evidence blocks live adoption regardless of a price-print result.

Screen gate: at least 100 completed trades on 100 distinct dates, zero unresolved exits, at least 51% strictly positive **base and stress net** trades, positive mean base and stress net, positive mean in both January–June and July–December for base and stress, and positive total after removing the five best trades. Show severe sensitivity, maximum loss and drawdown. Passing this 2025 screen would permit only an unchanged independent period test plus live bid/ask, fee, lot, tax and fillability checks; it would not authorize real orders. Compare with continuous holding before any capital allocation.

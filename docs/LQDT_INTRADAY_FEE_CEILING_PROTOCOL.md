# LQDT same-day feasibility screen — fixed before reading 2025 prices

LQDT is a liquid money-market fund candidate, but the T-Investments Investor tariff publicly lists **0.3% broker commission per side for funds**. This is a conditional tariff assumption, not confirmation of this user's per-order fee or any instrument-specific promotion. Before building a minute-level strategy, test whether a same-day move can even exceed the 0.6% broker-fee round trip. Source: MOEX ISS LQDT/TQTF daily candles for ordinary weekdays in 2025; keep source response and hash. Do not assume zero spread, zero slippage, or that any historical candle price was executable in real size.

For every valid day with a positive daily low and high, calculate an **impossible best-case** long outcome: buy at that day's low, sell at that day's high, ignore their time order, spread, slippage and tax, but charge 0.3% on both sides multiplicatively: `high*(1-.003)/(low*(1+.003))-1`. Count days with strictly positive oracle net. The same-day best-case short would require borrowing and is outside this long-only screen. Report missing and invalid days separately; never count them as losing days. No parameter sweep.

If fewer than 100 valid days can produce a positive oracle net, 2025 cannot supply 100 long winners even with hindsight under this tariff. If fewer than 51% of valid active days can, an unconditional daily long cannot reach the 51% net-win bar; selective trading is **not** ruled out. Any stronger conclusion requires independently fixed entry criteria and actual bid/ask costs. Sources: [T-Investments tariff](https://www.tbank.ru/invest/help/brokerage/account/get-bs/tariff/), [MOEX ISS](https://www.moex.com/a2920).

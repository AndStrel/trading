# T 10:31 entry cost ceiling — exploratory diagnostic, not a strategy

The reproducible ignored local report `.trading/t-intraday-cost-ceiling-2024-2025.json` has SHA-256 `3b3488cade3d6ea4cbbc2dd5685542d45c791839ea1084490ce17062ba39c815`. It uses [2024 TCSG→T](T_2024_SOURCE_CHECKPOINT.md) and [2025 T](T_2025_SOURCE_CHECKPOINT.md) public one-minute trade-candle archives. The calculation includes only days with every minute from 10:31 through 16:55 present: 192 of 207 candidate weekdays in 2024, 249 of 261 in 2025.

For each complete day, hypothetically buy at the 10:31 minute open and either sell at the 16:55 open or, for a deliberately **clairvoyant and non-executable upper bound**, sell at the best later one-minute high from 10:32 through 16:54. The latter uses future information, assumes a fill at a trade high and ignores queue position, so it is **not** a backtested trading rule or a realizable return. Both cases include 0.05% adverse execution per side, observed-grid adverse rounding, and either the current Investor 0.30% brokerage commission per side or a conditional zero-commission Pro scenario before subscription cost.

Under Investor, the scheduled same-day exit was net positive on only **41/192 days (21.35%) in 2024** and **50/249 (20.08%) in 2025**; mean net was −0.77682% and −0.73858%. Even the unattainable best-later-high upper bound was net positive on just **91/192 (47.40%)** and **96/249 (38.55%)**. Thus an *unconditional* 10:31 long entry cannot meet the user's 51% net-win requirement under these modeled Investor costs even with a clairvoyant exit. This does **not** rule out selective-entry rules on a subset of dates, other entry times, or different instruments.

Under conditional Pro brokerage-fee relief, the scheduled exit was positive on **73/192 (38.02%)** and **102/249 (40.96%)**, while the clairvoyant bound was positive on **162/192 (84.38%)** and **218/249 (87.55%)**. This large gap says exit timing could matter if Pro is genuinely available, but it provides **no predictive exit rule**. Pro status, its subscription cost, historic/live spreads, real fills and taxes remain unverified. Do not choose a strategy because of the oracle result. No orders were placed.

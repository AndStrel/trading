# Si US-session v1 — rejection-only missing-exit bound

This bound is fixed after the [timestamp checkpoint](SI_2026_EVENING_SOURCE_CHECKPOINT.md) and **before reading the candidate's price outcomes**. Apply the unchanged [v1 signal, fills and Investor-tariff costs](SI_US_SESSION_CONTINUATION_V1.md) to all 133 ordinary dates. Cash days with exactly zero first-half-hour return are not trades. Missing 16:30–16:59 signal minutes are data gaps; missing 17:01 entry cancels; a signaled date lacking the 22:00 print is an unresolved exit, never an observed win.

For the win-rate **upper bound only**, count every unresolved exit as a win, however implausibly favorable that may be. Denominator is all entered nonzero-signal dates, not just paired-print dates. Report gross/base/stress completed outcomes separately, both contracts and directions, mean completed-only return clearly labeled as selected, and unresolved count. If the upper bound is below 51% under base or stress, reject this exact rule. If it is at least 51%, the result is inconclusive and fails the zero-unresolved gate; do not claim an accepted strategy or calculate an unbiased aggregate mean without the missing exit price. Do not retune the exit minute or remove 11 June after seeing the result.

# TMON same-day carry v2 — earlier last-hour liquidation

Exploratory rule fixed **before evaluating the 16:00–17:00 exit coverage or any v2 price outcomes**. The 2025 v1 and 2026 timestamp checks showed many missing trade prints in the narrow 16:55–17:00 window. This v2 changes exit timing because of those observed **timestamps**, so it is not independent of the data's liquidity pattern. It must not be called a validated strategy without a later untouched period and contemporaneous quote/fill evidence. No orders.

Source: cached public MOEX ISS TMON one-minute trade candles for ordinary weekdays in 2025 (TQTF) and 2026-01-01 through 2026-09-25 (TQTF before 2026-06-22, TQBR thereafter). Preserve and verify raw hashes. For each active date, buy at the **open of the first trade-candle 10:01–10:03**; if absent, cancel. Sell at the **open of the first trade-candle 16:00–17:00 on that same date**; if absent, mark unresolved. The entry decision cannot use future exit availability. One long attempt per day, no leverage or planned overnight position. A trade print is not a quote or guaranteed fill.

Costs are frozen as in v1: zero broker fee **conditionally** on T-Investments TMON terms, adverse price allowance 0.005% per side base, 0.010% stress, 0.030% severe. Apply to entry/exit prices multiplicatively and adverse-round to provisional 0.000001 RUB tick. Fund expenses are embedded in prices, but actual broker tariff, spread, market impact, lot/tick history and taxes must be verified separately.

Before reading prices, perform timestamp-only coverage on both periods. If either has any entered date without a print in the exit window, do not score complete pairs as an unbiased strategy result; only a predeclared missing-exit bound may reject the rule. If both periods have **zero unresolved exits**, calculate each separately with no retuning. Required screen in **each** period: at least 100 completed trades on 100 dates, at least 51% strictly positive base **and** stress net trades, positive mean base and stress net, positive means in each half of the period, and positive total after removing five best trades. Show severe sensitivity, drawdown and continuous-hold comparison. Historical trade prints alone never authorize live orders.

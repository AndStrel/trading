# LQDT carry persistence v1: historical result and economic rejection

**The 51% historical win-frequency target is exceeded, but the rolling-trade strategy is not recommended.** The [rule](WEEKLY_LQDT_CARRY_V1.md) was frozen before outcomes, SHA-256 `6528a502e65886a2b840c3c553e8f50bc135fadf84c9d7d425000b5dda49107f`. Full audit: `.trading/weekly-lqdt-carry-v1.json`. Source: MOEX ISS TQTF LQDT daily candles 2022-07-22–2025-12-30, 919 source rows, row SHA-256 `30735af9d50b40756b1dcb0446671c61cb0f265d6aa3e2efff59dc8e72b5d519`. Weekend sessions are excluded by the engine. TQTF has no 2020–2021 LQDT candles, so this test cannot establish performance in low-rate regimes. 2026 was not used.

The entry signal required the **prior completed 20 eligible sessions** to appreciate more than the exact modeled round-trip cost, price ratio `1.007024575477307`. Buy at the next first-weekday-session open, sell at the open 20 eligible sessions later. Base costs were 0.3% commission plus 0.05% slippage on each side, with 0.1% slippage stress. Separate portfolios started at ₽1m for discovery (2023–2024), validation (2025), and the combined interval. End-of-period positions were marked and excluded from completed-trade wins.

In 2023–2024, **68/68 completed trades** earned a positive modeled net return; **70/72 active weeks** were positive. Mean completed-trade return was +0.537% base and +0.436% under slippage stress. The modeled portfolio ended at ₽1.075m with a 0.29% maximum marked drawdown; four positions were still pending at the cutoff. In the frozen 2025 validation, **48/48 completed trades** and **51/52 active weeks** were positive; mean net trade return was +0.761% base and +0.660% stress. The separate 2025 portfolio ended at ₽1.075m with four pending positions.

The combined 2023–2025 run had **120/120 completed winning trades** and **122/124 positive active weeks**, positive modeled ending equity **₽1.167m**, and 0.29% maximum marked drawdown. The sum of trade percentage returns without the five best was +71.97 percentage points. Annual completed-trade means were positive in 2023, 2024 and 2025. The smallest base-cost win was only **+0.082%**, and under higher slippage **119/120** trades remained winners (one became slightly negative), so a perfect base win record should not be extrapolated. No losing base-cost completed trades were observed, so the usual realized profit-factor denominator is zero: the report encodes it as `null`, and the gate treats an all-winning nonempty sample as an unbounded observed ratio, not a failure or a guarantee of future losses being impossible.

The economic comparison defeats the trading rule. A single modeled buy at the start of 2023 and sale at the end of 2025, with the same entry/exit cost assumptions, ended at **₽1.555m**, versus **₽1.167m** for repeated 20-session rolling trades. The continuous position also had **156/157 positive marked active weeks (99.36%)** with a 0.69% marked drawdown, versus 122/124 positive weeks and 0.29% drawdown for the rolling strategy. Its added return was much larger than its modest additional historical drawdown; this is an observed comparison, not a future risk guarantee. In 2025, the conditional rule and the always-long weekly control made exactly the same 48 completed trades; the filter added no selection value. Frequent round-trip costs and idle cash explain why an apparently perfect win rate can still be a poor choice. The one-time holding comparator is only one completed trade and therefore does not itself satisfy a 100-trade win-rate research gate.

The result is a **historical carry observation, not a forecasting edge or a trade authorization**. It covers unusually remunerative 2023–2025 conditions and one 2025 holdout, not a low-rate or fund-stress regime. Fund unit prices can fall; the Moscow Exchange explicitly says past returns do not determine future returns and does not guarantee fund income. The assumed 0.3% brokerage charge is consistent with the published *starting* Investor tariff for shares but has **not** been verified for this user's actual tariff and LQDT transaction type. Fund expenses are reflected in traded prices but have not been independently reconciled. Taxes, exact historical lot sizes, spread, actual opening liquidity/fills, premium/discount to NAV, and data-use rights need audit before any execution. No broker orders were sent.

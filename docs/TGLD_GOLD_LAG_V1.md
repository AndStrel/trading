# TGLD relative-to-gold lag v1 — locked before outcome

Research screening only; no orders. TGLD holds gold-related assets, but GLDRUB_TOM is a live proxy, **not** the fund's exact intraday NAV or an arbitrage guarantee. The fund's published performance indicator is the Bank of Russia's accounting price for a gram of gold. The hypothesis is that a material same-minute underperformance of the fund relative to gold sometimes corrects before the end of the calendar day.

Data: frozen public MOEX ISS TGLD one-minute trade candles in `.trading/tgld-2026-source/` (TQTF through 2026-06-19, TQBR from 2026-06-22), plus newly captured GLDRUB_TOM CETS one-minute price candles for exactly the same 133 ordinary weekdays 2026-03-23–2026-09-25. Gold `value`/`volume` fields may be null; this reference is price-only. No special-session dates. Use no future information.

For each day require both exact 10:00 candles; their closes form the ratio baseline. From 10:15 through 14:00 MSK, at each minute with a TGLD trade candle and a gold candle, calculate `goldClose/gold10Close - tgldClose/tgld10Close`. The first time this difference is at least **0.25%** and gold's own change since 10:00 is nonnegative is the only signal of the day. Enter long TGLD at the open of its first traded minute 1–2 minutes after that completed signal; if there is no such trade, cancel the day and do not seek another signal. Never short. Exit at the open of the first TGLD traded minute at or after 16:55, no later than 17:00; if absent, mark unresolved. No position overnight and no stop is assumed from sparse prints. Audit worst and tail losses explicitly.

Broker commission is modeled as 0 for the first 15 TGLD applications in a day under T-Invest's current published terms; the proposed one entry and one exit use two, but other account activity could consume the allowance. Base adverse execution allowance is **0.10% per side**, stress **0.20% per side**. Round the adverse buy price *up* and sell price *down* to the observed 0.01 RUB TGLD price tick before calculating returns. These are assumptions, not measured historical spread or fills. Fund expenses are embedded in observed prices. The 0.25% signal threshold is fixed above the 0.20% modeled base round-trip execution allowance before tick rounding; do not tune it on this archive.

Admission: at least 100 completed trades on at least 50 dates, at least 51% base-net profitable trades, positive mean and aggregate base net, positive stress mean, positive March–May and June–September blocks, no material unresolved cases, acceptable tail loss and risk controls, independent 2025 validation, and live bid/ask/paper-fill confirmation before any real order. Any positive historical print-based outcome remains exploratory until the last two checks.

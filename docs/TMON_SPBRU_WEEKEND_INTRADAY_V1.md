# TMON@/SPBRU weekend intraday v1 — frozen before price outcomes

Research only, no orders. This is a **new venue-specific question**, not validation of the rejected TMON/TQTF–TQBR weekday rule. T-Invest identifies TMON@/SPBRU (UID `498ec3ff-ef27-4729-9703-a5aac48d5789`) and TMON/TQBR as the same ISIN `RU000A106DL2` but different trading modes and position UIDs. The SPBRU mode currently has API trading enabled. A read-only 2026-09-28 SPBRU order book showed a one-kopeck spread, and the account-specific preliminary cost of one buy and one sell limit order showed zero brokerage commission; these are one-time observations, not guarantees of future quotes, fees or fills.

**Mechanism to falsify:** weekend trading may gradually reprice a money-market fund during a calendar day even while the underlying money market is mostly closed. This is not assured: weekend accrual might be priced before the entry, only after exit, or offset by market-maker spread and risk. Test only Saturday and Sunday dates from **2025-03-01 through 2026-09-27**, inclusive. The 2025 portion is discovery; the entire 2026 portion is unchanged-rule temporal confirmation. No weekday selection, thresholds or alternate exit times may be chosen after seeing outcomes.

**Source checkpoint before returns:** request T-Invest `GetCandles` for TMON@/SPBRU, exchange-source one-hour candles. Persist raw returned candle pages and their SHA-256 hashes locally. Count dates, missing 11:00 and 17:00 Moscow candles, zero-volume candles and incomplete intervals **without reading prices or returns**. Do not silently discard inactive or missing dates. The selected entry/exit hours were fixed after timestamp-only checks of 2026-09-26 and 2026-09-27; no price outcomes from those days were inspected.

**One attempt per weekend date:** schedule the buy attempt for 11:00 MSK on **every** weekend date, before knowing whether that hour will contain a trade. Use the **open of the 11:00–11:59 MSK exchange candle** (08:00 UTC) as a print proxy if the completed candle exists and has positive volume; otherwise cancel the day after the hour without searching for another entry. Sell at the **open of the 17:00–17:59 MSK exchange candle** (14:00 UTC) on the same date. If a position was entered but that exit candle is absent or has zero volume, mark the exit unresolved and do not count the date as a completed win. Never short, lever, retry or deliberately hold overnight. Both entry and exit are trade-print proxies, not contemporaneous executable quotes; the hour label is not the exact first-trade timestamp, and an opening print does not prove that an order placed at the hour would fill.

**Frozen costs:** zero broker commission conditionally on the read-only 2026-09-28 one-lot preliminary quote; fund expenses are already embedded in market prices. Use the observed 0.01-RUB price grid. Base buy = entry print + **0.01 RUB**, base sell = exit print − **0.01 RUB**. Stress uses **0.02 RUB per side**. Positive means strictly sell/buy − 1 > 0 after those allowances. Separately show gross print return, loss tails, concentration without best five dates, and a marked-path risk proxy; taxes and account-specific realized fees remain unverified. Do not interpret one recent one-tick book as a historical spread series.

**Admission screen:** before scoring, reject the return test or use only a predeclared rejection bound if any entered date lacks an exit; never score only observable pairs as an unbiased sample. With zero unresolved exits, require at least **100 completed trades across at least 100 dates combined**, at least **50 completed trades in each calendar-year block**, at least **51% strictly base-net winners and positive base mean in each block**, positive combined stress mean, positive combined sum without the five best trades, and acceptable worst-date and marked-path risk. A pass remains exploratory until a further untouched period and prospective SPBRU bid/ask plus paper-fill evidence support actual execution. A fail rejects this exact rule; no retuning against the same archives. No order is authorized by this protocol.

# TGLD morning-selloff reversal v1 — frozen before outcomes

Research only; no orders. Distinct from the rejected gold-relative lag rule: this tests whether a fund-specific 30-minute selloff tends to reverse during the rest of the same day. This does **not** assume a stable gold price or exact intraday NAV. The 2025 TQTF one-minute trade-candle archive is discovery; 2026, already partly inspected in other research, is a weaker confirmation sample. No parameter changes after reading either result.

For every active 2025 weekday, require exact 10:00 and 10:30 TGLD one-minute candles. Compare their **closes**. If `close10:30 / close10:00 - 1 <= -0.002`, signal one long entry. Buy at the **open** of the first traded TGLD minute 10:31–10:33. If none, cancel the day without searching again. If the selloff condition is false, remain in cash. Sell at the open of the first traded minute 16:55–17:00, never carry overnight. If there is no such print, mark exit unresolved and count as a failure of the same-day-exit requirement, not as a winning trade. No intraday stop is inferred from sparse prints; report adverse tail and do not regard the model as deployable without a real-time exit plan.

The current T-Invest published offer gives TGLD 15 applications per day without broker commission across eligible accounts; model zero broker commission only under that allowance. Two applications are proposed, but other activity could consume it. Base adverse execution is 0.10% **per side**, stress 0.20% per side. Round buy up and sell down to the MOEX TGLD 0.01 RUB tick, then calculate round-trip returns. This is an assumption rather than measured historical spread/fills. Fund expenses are already embedded in traded price. Taxes are not modeled, so any apparent pass is still provisional.

Discovery admission: at least 100 completed trades on 50 dates; at least 51% **base-net** winners; positive base mean, aggregate, and stress mean; positive mean in both January–June and July–December; positive total after removing the five best trades; no unresolved exits; tail-loss/risk review. Do not look at 2026 strategy outcome unless 2025 discovery passes these gates. Even then, an unchanged 2026 check and forward bid/ask paper-fill validation are mandatory before considering execution.
